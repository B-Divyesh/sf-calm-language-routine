Privacy

Last updated August 28, 2026.

Your study data stays on your device

Quiet Loop stores cards, archive reasons, plans, sessions, and optional reflections in your browser’s local IndexedDB. It does not create an account and does not send that study data to Quiet Loop or a third party.

Exports and deletion

You can export your cards as CSV or a full JSON backup from About. “Delete all local data” clears Quiet Loop’s browser storage on that device. Clearing browser site data has the same effect.

Purchase unlock

If you choose the optional Quiet Shelf unlock, a license token is stored in this browser’s local storage. Quiet Loop may contact Sociobot’s license-verification endpoint at most once daily to check that token. Sociobot/Dodo processes the purchase as merchant of record; its checkout privacy terms apply there.

No tracking

Quiet Loop has no advertising, behavioral analytics, third-party runtime scripts, or external fonts. The app can be used offline after its first load.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator through the product listing at sociobot.in.
